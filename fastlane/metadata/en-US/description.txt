Pocket Dolphin · DSH connects to the DSH host you choose, so you can continue your work from iPhone and iPad.

• Pair with your own DSH host
• Browse workspaces and sessions
• Read and continue conversations
• Use the same host across your devices

Pocket Dolphin · DSH is free and open source. It is an unofficial client for DeepSeek Harness and is not affiliated with DeepSeek.

Source code: https://github.com/baixianger/dsh-ios
