Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Pocket Dolphin · DSH is a client for a DSH host that you choose and operate. This policy describes the app’s data handling. The policy of your chosen DSH host operator also applies to any data sent to that host.

No tracking or advertising

Pocket Dolphin · DSH has no user accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics, telemetry, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with data brokers.

What stays on your device

The app stores the minimum configuration needed to reconnect to your chosen host:

  • Host names, connection addresses, and the currently selected host.
  • Workspace and session list snapshots used to make the sidebar available and responsive.
  • Appearance and app preferences.
  • Pairing and refresh credentials in the iOS Keychain. They are not stored in the app’s ordinary preferences.

You can remove a host and its credentials from the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data; iOS Keychain retention behavior is controlled by the operating system.

Data sent to your DSH host

When you connect, Pocket Dolphin · DSH communicates directly with the DSH host address you configure or pair. This can include authentication credentials, workspace and session metadata, prompts, responses, and any content you deliberately submit. If you select an image or use voice input, that content is handled for the requested feature and sent only as part of the interaction with your chosen host.

Pocket Dolphin · DSH does not route this information through an impai.me server or another Pocket Dolphin · DSH-operated relay.

Device permissions

  • Camera: only to scan a one-time QR code for pairing with your DSH host.
  • Microphone: only when you choose voice input.
  • Photos: only when you explicitly select an image to attach. The system photo picker limits access to the selected item.

Your host and external services

The host you pair may be on your LAN, Tailnet, or a custom address. That host may itself call models, tools, or services according to its own configuration. Pocket Dolphin · DSH does not control those services or their privacy practices.

Children

Pocket Dolphin · DSH is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
